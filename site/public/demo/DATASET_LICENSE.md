# Demo dataset

The street scene was generated for this project with OpenAI image generation and is synthetic. The twelve images are crops of the same generated scene, not a real recorded journey. Coordinates are invented near 0° latitude and longitude in the Gulf of Guinea and do not represent a surveyed route. The synthetic images and coordinate files may be redistributed under CC0 1.0. They contain no private source recording or GPS data. Map tiles, when requested, are supplied separately by OpenStreetMap and carry their own attribution and usage terms.
