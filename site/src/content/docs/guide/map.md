---
title: Map, photos and corrections
---

Select a point or use Previous photo and Next photo. Dots become directional arrows when zoomed in, where a bearing is available. Choose OpenStreetMap in the layer control for a street background.

In the survey workspace, select a photo point and choose **Edit**. Enter coordinates and bearing or drag the handle, then save. To delete a point and its photo from the current revision, select **Delete point** and confirm. The route reconnects neighboring points within the same recording. Corrections are applied to matching observations after reprocessing.

For rotated photographs, select **Rotate exported photos** and process again. Published maps are read-only and retain their previously published revision until republished.
