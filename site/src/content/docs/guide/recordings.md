---
title: Multiple recordings and exports
---

Open a completed survey and choose **Add recording**. Upload a ZIP or matching MP4 and LOG, validate, and process. Each recording keeps its own video timeline and route segment.

Download `survey.zip` for images and GIS outputs with relative image paths. Individual outputs include CSV, point and route GeoJSON, and a point GeoPackage. Coordinates use WGS 84 (EPSG:4326). Open the GeoPackage or GeoJSON in QGIS using its vector layer import. GPS accuracy and sampling influence positional quality.
