# Community conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to work. Treat other people with respect, be constructive when discussing ideas, and respect privacy and boundaries. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when conduct harms the community. Report concerns privately through the repository's private vulnerability reporting channel when enabled, or through another private contact method established by the owner. Avoid posting personal information in public issues.
